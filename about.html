About Us

Brass Flow Faucets is a trading company based in Kaiping, Guangdong Province, China — home of the country's largest faucet manufacturing cluster.

How we work

We are not a middleman guessing at prices. We work hand-in-hand with production partner factories in Shuikou, Kaiping, where brass casting, CNC machining, polishing, plating and assembly all sit within a few kilometres of each other. Our team coordinates production, quality checks, packing and shipping directly at the factory floor.

We are transparent about our model: we are a trading company with partner factories. That means you get one English-speaking contact who answers within 24 hours, and factory-direct pricing without the language barrier, quality surprises or payment risks of dealing alone.

Who we serve

Importers, wholesalers, plumbing-supply distributors, hotel and residential project contractors — mainly across the Middle East, Africa, Latin America and Southeast Asia. We support both stock items and private-label (OEM/ODM) programs.

Our commitments

• Pre-shipment inspection photos and videos with every order.
• Video factory tours on request — see the production line before you commit.
• Sample orders welcome; sample cost credited against bulk orders.
• One contact from inquiry to after-sales — WhatsApp, email, same person.
